  • Entry: price crosses the previous week's high (gap-ups fill at the open). One entry attempt per stock per week.
  • Each buy, whether entry or averaging, uses one slot. Averaging levels are measured from the original entry price.
  • Daily candles: no exit or averaging on the entry day; if a day touches both a stop and the target, the stop is taken first; if it touches the target and an averaging level, the exit is taken first.
  • The pool is today's top stocks applied to past years, so results are flattered by survivorship. Prices are split and dividend adjusted.
